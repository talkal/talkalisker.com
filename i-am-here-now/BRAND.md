# I Am Here Now — Brand Definition

Emotional Integration workshops by Tal Kalisker, rooted in Michael Brown's *The Presence Process*.

## Essence

**One line:** A guided journey from body to breath to feeling — arriving fully in the present moment.

**The name** comes from the practice itself: the six-word breath mantra spoken on the circular breath — *I* (inhale), *am* (exhale), *here* (inhale), *now* (exhale), *in* (inhale), *this* (exhale). The brand name keeps the first four words; the full mantra appears wherever the practice is described.

## Audience

Festival curators and workshop programmers (Mycelia, Compartires, and similar). Secondary: attendees deciding which workshop to join. Both need to feel, within seconds, that this is a serious, embodied, safely-held practice — not vague new-age content.

## Voice

- **Grounded, not mystical.** Speak plainly about the body, breath, and emotion. "Energy in motion," not "vibrations."
- **Warm and direct.** Second person, present tense. Short sentences that breathe.
- **Credible.** Lineage (The Presence Process), lived experience (5 full journeys through the process, 8 years Vijnana yoga), festival track record.
- Avoid: healing-industry clichés ("transform your life"), exclamation marks, jargon without explanation.

## Visual identity

**Feeling:** dawn light, warm earth, stillness with movement inside it. The moment just after a deep exhale.

### Typography

- **Display:** Fraunces (variable optical serif) — warm, human, slightly soft. Light weights, generous size.
- **Body/UI:** Karla — humanist sans, open and unfussy.
- Wordmark: "I am here now" set lowercase in Fraunces italic, with interpunct rhythm: *I · am · here · now*.

### Motifs

- **The breath rhythm**: the mantra words separated by interpuncts (·) as a recurring rhythm element.
- **Concentric/radiating soft circles**: the breath, the circle of practitioners.
- **Grain and warmth**: photography in golden-hour tones, film-like, documentary rather than stocky.

### Imagery direction

Real-feeling documentary photography: festival shade structures, circles of practitioners, yoga movement at dawn, hands on heart. Warm terracotta/amber/sand palette, soft natural light, no neon, no lens flares, no posed smiling-at-camera.

## Page structure (landing)

1. Hero — name, one-line promise, festival credibility, CTA
2. What it is — emotional integration in plain words
3. The journey — three movements: Body (yoga) → Mind (theory & technique) → Breath (practice) → closing with the inner child
4. The breath — the mantra visualized
5. Teacher — Tal's lineage and experience (Tal teaches a tool; "facilitator" is not used)
6. Voices — testimonials
7. Booking CTA — WhatsApp + email
