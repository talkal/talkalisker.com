The practice

Emotion is energy in motion

When emotion can't move through us, it stays: as charge, as tension, as patterns that repeat. Emotional integration lets that energy flow again.

This workshop is a complete, self-contained journey. We prepare the body with yoga, understand the technique with clear teaching, then practice conscious connected breathing. The breath is circular, audible and strong, carried by six words: I am here now in this.

The breath brings us into presence. Presence lets charged emotion surface, move, and integrate. No experience needed, only willingness to feel.

Based on the teachings of Michael Brown in The Presence Process.

The journey

Three movements, one arrival

01 · Physical body

Move & open

We begin on the mat. Yoga sequences drawn from eight years of Vijnana practice warm the body, soften its holding, and open the pathways energy moves through. The body becomes ready to feel.

02 · Mental body

Understand

Clear, grounded teaching: what emotional integration is, why charged emotion gets stored, and exactly how the breathing technique works. You'll know what you're doing and why before the first breath.

03 · Emotional body

Breathe

Circular connected breathing, strong enough to hear, carried by the mantra: I on the inhale, am on the exhale, and on through here, now, in, this. The breath makes us present, and what is felt fully can finally move.

The landing

Sitting with what moves, meeting the inner child

We close in stillness: sitting with the energy in motion, letting it settle where it belongs, and turning gently toward the inner child, the one who first felt what we've been carrying. Participants leave grounded, soft, and present.

The breath

Six words. One rhythm.

Each word rides one breath, spoken inwardly as the air moves. Follow the circle: it breathes at the pace of the practice.

Teacher

Tal Kalisker

I'm 29, and emotional integration changed my life. The hard feelings suddenly had meaning, and in integrating them I've grown beyond anything I imagined. Today I'm better for myself and for the people around me, and I can actually be present.

I don't teach this from a book. I teach it from the inside, having moved through the full ten-week Presence Process five times, each journey deepening both the practice and my capacity to hold others in it. The workshop weaves that lived experience with eight years of Vijnana yoga, a practice of listening to the body from within, into a session that is safe, clear, and complete in itself.

I give this workshop because the practice gave me so much that I want to give it back to the world.
