# 01. /executive_summary

> Extensive strategy session defining Voira's core market positioning, target audience profile, and creative brand direction. The consensus is to decouple Voira from generic, technology-first AI consultancies by leading with a deeply human, partnership-driven identity. This mitigates the acute "consultancy fatigue" and AI anxiety in the target market. The brand will launch utilizing the "Flow/Trusted Partner" visual architecture to align seamlessly with the Rapinformes partnership.

- **Decision:** Meeting Attendees Confirmed | **Impact:** Medium

# 03. /market_landscape
The Spanish AI market is highly aware but executing poorly. Voira's opportunity lies in structural implementation, not basic tool training.

- **Metric:** Spanish SME AI Adoption | **Value:** 61% | **Trend:** +7% above EU
AWS 2026 report indicates high integration, but execution remains shallow (predominantly basic chatbots).

- **Metric:** Productivity Acknowledgment | **Value:** 86% | **Trend:** Stable
ADO group reports Spanish SMEs already know they need AI. Voira does not need to educate; it needs to execute.

- **Finding:** Subsidized Market Saturation | **Severity:** Medium
The Spanish government's 24,000 EUR "Kit Digital" voucher programs will flood the market with low-tier agencies. Voira's partnership with Rapinformes is the critical silver bullet to bypass noise and establish trust.

# 04. /audience_profile
Voira targets ambitious but operationally trapped founders who require digital transformation without operational collapse.

- **Finding:** Severe Capacity Ceilings | **Severity:** High
Targeting 5-50 employee SMEs in Madrid (Law, Compliance, Accounting, Healthcare). Workflows are choked by manual data entry. Growth is restricted to linear headcount increases.

- **Finding:** High AI Anxiety | **Severity:** Critical
The audience is terrified of systemic risks and operational crashes. Trust is highly relational, requiring formal etiquette and deep networking. Cost-cutters and non-digital product companies are explicitly excluded as anti-audiences.

# 05. /competitive_positioning
The market offers two extremes: generic tool trainers or massive enterprise consultancies.

- **Finding:** The Enterprise R&D Trap | **Severity:** High
Competitors (Deloitte, Bain, Accenture) embed teams that function as a $200k+ ongoing tax. Voira's $10k-$20k one-time implementation offers superior ROI by fixing digital infrastructure before building custom AI agents.

- **Finding:** Tech-First Alienation | **Severity:** Medium
Agencies like 10 Pearls and Launch Consulting alienate conservative SMEs with tech-heavy jargon. Voira wins by being accessible.

# 06. /creative_directions
Yaeli presented two distinct visual and naming territories for the Voira brand based on strategic documentation.

- **Name:** Direction 1: Structure & Order | **Slogan:** Intelligent Framework | **Level:** Backup
Projects rigor, reliability, and institutional trust to make businesses immune to scaling chaos. Visuals are tech-forward, systematic, and stable (neon palette). Naming options: Structa, Ordo, Synfra, Cadex, Ascensio. Highly reliable but potentially too cold for initial launch.

- **Name:** Direction 2: Flow | **Slogan:** Trusted Partner | **Level:** Recommended
Focuses on human connection, unblocked momentum, and side-by-side transition. Visuals use soft, rounded shapes, human imagery, and warmer colors. Naming options: Novera, Compás, Astrol, Nexum, Alapar, Acorde. Perfectly mirrors Rapinformes' marketing and provides psychological safety.

# 07. /decisions
- **Decision:** Adopt "Flow / Trusted Partner" Direction | **Impact:** High
Aligns Voira as an approachable guide. Structure direction is reserved for future technical engineering divisions.

- **Decision:** Reject Synfra Brand Name | **Impact:** Medium
Phonetic flow is clunky in Spanish. Name saved for future hosting tools.

- **Decision:** Select Top Naming Candidates | **Impact:** High
Voira strongly favored Novera and Alapar (Flow), alongside Ordo and Structa (Structure) for legal review.

- **Decision:** Integrate Carmen into Sales | **Impact:** Medium
Carmen will collaborate with Tal on direct outreach to Rapinformes clients.
