# 01. /executive_summary

> This report outlines the core brand identity, tone of voice, and taglines for Voira. Voira targets operationally mature Spanish SMEs in conservative sectors (law, compliance, healthcare), offering structural and technological modernization with a focus on deep, continuous accompaniment.

# 02. /core_identity

- **Finding:** The Strategic Positioning | **Severity:** Info
Voira operates on a hybrid positioning model: warm in the relationship, rigorous in the design of the systems. The target is operationally mature Spanish SMEs (5–50 employees) in conservative, knowledge-intensive sectors like law, compliance, and healthcare. These founders are growth-oriented but have hit a capacity ceiling due to legacy technological systems.

- **Finding:** The Value Proposition | **Severity:** Info
Voira offers structural, technological modernization for leaders suffering from deep consulting fatigue. Instead of just delivering advisory reports, Voira provides deep, continuous accompaniment through the transformation process. Revenue growth is decoupled from linear headcount increases by guiding companies toward a frictionless digital infrastructure.

- **Finding:** Visual Architecture | **Severity:** Info
The visual identity utilizes the "Trusted Partner". The aesthetic is sophisticated but warm, projecting a premium B2B feeling through structured typography, restraint, generous whitespace, and a confident palette. Neon circuit boards, corporate stiffness, and soft consumer-lifestyle aesthetics are actively avoided.

# 03. /tone_of_voice

- **Finding:** Professional & Rigorous | **Severity:** Info
Voira speaks with the institutional weight and exactness demanded by highly regulated sectors.

- **Finding:** Human & Accessible | **Severity:** Info
Voira acts as an approachable guide, proving to be "alongside you, not above you," and providing psychological safety.

- **Finding:** Copywriting Laws | **Severity:** High
Eliminate tech-heavy jargon that alienates conservative SMEs. Emphasize guided implementation—accompanying the team step-by-step to guarantee structural order. Technology must be framed as a tool, not the hero. Voira is the trusted architect guiding "Zero-Downtime Structural Upgrades."
